# Termco website surface

## Purpose

Explain Termco to developers through real product evidence, lead them to the correct current download, and provide usable product documentation.

## Audience and decision

The primary visitor is a developer deciding whether Termco can replace a fragmented daily workflow without surrendering control. A secondary visitor is an engineering lead evaluating composition, remote work, and team profiles.

## Structure

The homepage is a terminal-led story: identify the cost of rebuilding context, show the unified workspace, tour agents, Git, Preview, remote work, and plugins through real media, then expose the toolkit, installation choices, and common questions.

## First viewport

The header, concise one-window thesis, platform-aware download, source link, and live terminal must be immediately understandable. The complete workspace capture follows without cropping. The page should identify itself as a developer workspace, not an AI chat product or generic terminal.

## Behavior

Download actions resolve the latest GitHub release for the detected platform and offer explicit platform choices. The hero terminal types and boots once. Videos may autoplay only when permitted, remain muted, and pause off-screen. Reduced motion receives static terminal output and no autoplay.

## Truth and constraints

Use only current capabilities documented in PRODUCT.md and the supplied product presentation. No customer logos, testimonials, pricing, benchmarks, or unreleased claims. Do not push before local user review.
